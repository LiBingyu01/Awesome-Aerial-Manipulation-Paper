## Legend & Scope

- **Published vs. preprint:** Conference/journal labels follow the collected publication information. *arXiv preprint* is not an acceptance venue; consult the final publisher record for updates.
- **Three takeaways:** Editorial summaries of the task, method, and technical value. They are not verbatim or necessarily the authors’ own numbered claims.
- **Scope:** Closely related work on aerial grippers, hooks, suspended payloads, cooperative transport, and interactive benchmarks is included alongside articulated aerial manipulators.
